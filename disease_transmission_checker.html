Key Prevention Measures

Simple precautions drastically cut down both direct and indirect disease transmission.

1. Personal Hygiene

  • Wash hands vigorously for at least 20 seconds with soap and warm water.
  • Use alcohol-based hand sanitizer if soap is unavailable.
  • Minimize touching your mouth, nose, or eyes with unwashed hands.
  • Cover coughs/sneezes and wash hands immediately afterward.
  • Wear gloves when handling blood, feces, or caring for ill persons.

2. Food & Water Safety

  • Prevent cross-contamination by keeping raw meats separate from produce.
  • Set refrigerator to ≤ 40°F (4°C) and freezer to ≤ 0°F (-18°C).
  • Cook meats to 145°F (63°C), ground meat to 160°F (71°C), poultry to 165°F (73°C).
  • Avoid untreated tap water when traveling in high-risk zones.

3. Insects & Animals

  • Wear long sleeves and long pants in wooded or high-insect areas.
  • Use insect repellent and mosquito netting.
  • Check for ticks immediately after outdoor activities; remove with fine tweezers.
  • Avoid direct contact with wild, sick, or dead animals.

4. Vaccinations

  • Stay up to date on vaccines (Measles, Mumps, Influenza, HPV, etc.).
  • Keep pet vaccinations current to prevent zoonotic outbreaks.
  • Consult your doctor prior to international travel for specific immunizations.
